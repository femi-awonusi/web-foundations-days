About QuickNotes

QuickNotes is a simple note-taking application designed to help you capture and organise your ideas. It allows you to record important thoughts, manage tasks and keep track of information in one place.

How to use QuickNotes

  1. Open the Home page to access your notes.
  2. Enter your idea or task in the New note input field.
  3. Click the Add Note button to add your note to the list.
  4. Review your notes in the Your notes section.

Features

  • Quickly capture ideas and important information.
  • Organise tasks and personal reminders.
  • View your notes in a simple list.
  • Easy-to-use and beginner-friendly interface.
  • Keyboard shortcuts for convenient navigation.

Keyboard shortcuts

Shortcut Action
Ctrl + Enter Submit a new note
Esc Clear the current input
Tab Move to the next interactive element
